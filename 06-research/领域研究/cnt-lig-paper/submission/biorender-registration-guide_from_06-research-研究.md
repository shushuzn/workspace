## 📋 Registration Steps

### Step 1: Visit BioRender Website

**URL:** https://biorender.com/

**Action:**
- Open browser (Chrome/Edge recommended)
- Navigate to biorender.com
- Wait for homepage to load

**Time:** 30 seconds

---

### Step 2: Click Sign Up

**Action:**
- Click "Sign Up" button (top right corner)
- Or click "Get Started" (center of page)

**Time:** 10 seconds

---

### Step 3: Choose Plan

**Options:**

**Option A: Free Plan (Recommended for trial)**
- ✅ 30-day free trial
- ✅ Access to 1000+ icons
- ✅ Basic export (PNG, low resolution)
- ❌ No TIFF export
- ❌ No commercial use

**Option B: Scientific Plan ($39/month)**
- ✅ All Free Plan features
- ✅ TIFF export (300 dpi)
- ✅ SVG export
- ✅ Commercial use allowed
- ✅ Priority support

**Recommendation:** Start with Free Plan, upgrade if needed

**Time:** 1 minute

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Issue: Email verification not received

**Solution:**
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 5 minutes
3. Request resend verification email
4. Try different email address

### Issue: Cannot create account

**Solution:**
1. Clear browser cache
2. Try incognito/private mode
3. Use different browser
4. Contact BioRender support: support@biorender.com

### Issue: Icons not showing

**Solution:**
1. Refresh page (F5)
2. Check internet connection
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try different browser

### Issue: Export fails

**Solution:**
1. Check file size (<10 MB)
2. Reduce canvas size
3. Try different format
4. Contact BioRender support
