### News Scoring System
```
Dimensions:
- Importance (0-40): critical/high/medium/low
- Impact (0-20): global/national/industry/company
- Credibility (0-20): based on news source
- Timeliness (0-20): breaking/today/week/old

Grades:
- S (80+): 🔴 Must read
- A (60-79): 🟠 Important
- B (40-59): 🟡 Normal
- C (20-39): 🟢 Reference
- D (<20): ⚪ Ignorable
```
