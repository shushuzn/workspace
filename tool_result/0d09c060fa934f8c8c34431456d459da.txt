### Technical Patterns Observed

1. **Windows asyncio**: Need `asyncio.WindowsSelectorEventLoopPolicy()` explicitly
2. **File write truncation**: `write_file` has ~8KB limit, use Python scripts for larger files
3. **Jin10 API**: xnews.jin10.com has embedded content in title field (not separate description)
4. **RSS parsing**: Use `<!\[CDATA\[...\]\]>` and regular `<title>` fallback
5. **RSS content**: Standard RSS only has 100-200 char descriptions, not full articles
