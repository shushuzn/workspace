## Do LLMs Benefit From Therir Own Words

# Abstract:
Multi-turn interactions with LLLs retain assigntant's past responses. Unexpectely, removing prior assistant responses does not affect response quality on a large fraction of turns.

# Key Findings:
- Multi-turn conversations consist of 6.4% self-contained prompts
- Omitting assistant-history can reduce cumulative context lengths by up to 10X
- Context pollution: models over-condition on previous responses

- Context-filtering approach selectively omits assistant history
