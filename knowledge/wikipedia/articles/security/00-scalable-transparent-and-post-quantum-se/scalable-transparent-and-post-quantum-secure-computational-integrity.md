# Scalable, transparent, and post-quantum secure computational integrity

**IACR ePrint** | ePrint: 2018/046 | **Author**: Eli Ben-Sasson, Iddo Bentov, Yinon Horesh, Michael Riabzev

## 摘要

Human dignity demands that personal information, like medical and forensic data, be hidden from the public. But veils of secrecy designed to preserve privacy may also be abused to cover up lies and deceit by parties entrusted with Data, unjustly harming citizens and eroding trust in central institutions. Zero knowledge (ZK) proof systems are an ingenious cryptographic solution to the tension between the ideals of personal privacy and institutional integrity, enforcing the latter in a way that does not compromise the former. Public trust demands transparency from ZK systems, meaning they be set up with no reliance on any trusted party, and have no trapdoors that could be exploited by powerful parties to bear false witness. For ZK systems to be used with Big Data, it is imperative that the public verification process scale sublinearly in data size. Transparent ZK proofs that can be verified exponentially faster than data size were first described in the 1990s but early constructions were impractical, and no ZK system realized thus far in code (including that used by crypto-currencies like Zcash) has achieved both transparency and exponential verification speedup, simultaneously, for general computations. Here we report the first realization of a transparent ZK system (ZK-STARK) in which verification scales exponentially faster than database size, and moreover, this exponential speedup in verification is observed concretely for meaningful and sequential computations, described next. Our system uses several recent advances on interactive oracle proofs (IOP), such as a “fast” (linear time) IOP system for error correcting codes. Our proof-of-concept system allows the Police to prove to the public that the DNA profile of a Presidential Candidate does not appear in the forensic DNA profile database maintained by the Police. The proof, which is generated by the Police, relies on no external trusted party, and reveals no further information about the contents of the database, nor about the candidate’s profile; in particular, no DNA information is disclosed to any party outside the Police. The proof is shorter than the size of the DNA database, and verified faster than the time needed to examine that database naively. ## 研究动机

零知识证明系统在个人隐私和机构诚信之间存在根本张力：

- **隐私与人格尊严**：医疗、执法等个人数据需要对公众保密
- **零知识解决张力**：ZK证明系统在保护隐私的同时强制执行机构诚信
- **透明性需求**：公众信任要求ZK系统必须透明设置，无信任方依赖，无后门
- **大数据挑战**：ZK系统要用于大数据，必须支持次线性验证

核心问题：能否同时实现透明性（无需信任方）和指数级验证加速？

## 核心方法

### 1. ZK-STARK首次实现

首个同时满足透明性和指数级验证加速的ZK系统：

- **透明设置**：无需任何可信方
- **无后门**：无trapdoor可被利用
- **指数级加速**：验证时间指数级快于数据大小
- **后量子安全**：基于交互式预言机证明（IOP）

### 2. 近期IOP进展利用

利用交互式预言机证明的最新进展：

- **线性时间IOP**：针对纠错码的快速IOP系统
- **快速Police DNA证明**：警察向公众证明候选人DNA不在数据库中
- **不泄露信息**：不透露数据库内容或候选人档案
- **证明比数据库更短**：体积小于原始DNA数据库

### 3. 实际应用示例

具体应用场景：

- **DNA档案验证**：证明候选人DNA不在执法档案中
- **无需可信方**：证明生成不依赖任何外部可信方
- **隐私保护**：除Police外不泄露任何DNA信息
- **验证比数据库检查更快**：指数级验证加速

### 4. 技术基础

系统使用的核心技术：

- **IOP范式**：交互式预言机证明框架
- **纠错码**：利用纠错码的线性时间IOP
- **后量子安全**：安全性基于IOP而非数论假设

## 关键发现

1. **首个透明ZK-STARK**：同时实现透明性和指数级验证加速
2. **后量子安全**：不受量子计算威胁
3. **无需信任设置**：突破传统SNARK需要信任设置的问题
4. **大数据适用**：次线性验证适用于大规模数据
5. **实际演示**：DNA验证案例证明实用价值

## 个人评价

ZK-STARK是零知识证明领域的重大突破，摆脱了对信任设置和数论假设的依赖。其透明性和后量子安全性使其特别适合需要长期安全保证的应用场景。

**深层跨域联系**：

1. **透明性 ↔ 拓扑开放性**：STARK的透明性与拓扑学中的开放性（openness）有概念相似——都是无需"隐藏"结构即可验证

2. **指数级加速 ↔ 拓扑维度跃升**：指数级验证加速与拓扑学中的维度跃升有深层联系——都涉及某种"超越线性"的质变

3. **IOP框架 ↔ 拓扑信息传递**：交互式预言机证明与拓扑学中的信息传递有概念相似——通过预言机中继信息，保持某种结构完整性
