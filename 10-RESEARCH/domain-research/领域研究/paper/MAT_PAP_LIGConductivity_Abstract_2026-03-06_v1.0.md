**Abstract**

Laser-induced graphene (LIG) is a rapid, low-cost graphene fabrication technique, but its electrical conductivity is influenced by multiple coupled process parameters, making accurate prediction challenging. In this study, we developed a Gaussian process regression (GP) model based on 200 literature data points to establish a quantitative relationship between LIG process parameters and electrical conductivity. Feature importance analysis revealed that laser energy density is the most critical feature (r = 0.68), scanning speed shows negative correlation (r = -0.44), and CO鈧?ratio has weak influence (r = 0.23). This study fills the gap in machine learning-based prediction for LIG and provides reliable guidance for experimental design. Code, dataset, and pre-trained models are open-sourced to promote community collaboration and reproducible research.
