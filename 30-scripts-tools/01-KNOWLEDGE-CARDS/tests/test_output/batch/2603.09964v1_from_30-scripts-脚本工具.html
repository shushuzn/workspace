📖 摘要

As social virtual reality (VR) grows more popular, addressing acces- sibility for blind and low vision (BLV) users is increasingly critical. Researchers have proposed an AI “sighted guide” to help users navi- gate VR and answer their questions, but it has not been studied with users. To address this gap, we developed a large language model (LLM)-powered guide and studied its use with 16 BLV participants in virtual environments with confederates posing as other users. For example, VRChat regularly hosts 40,000 players per month [61]. However, the complex nature of social VR, where key visual information changes frequently, makes it particularly challenging for blind and low vision (BLV) people.
