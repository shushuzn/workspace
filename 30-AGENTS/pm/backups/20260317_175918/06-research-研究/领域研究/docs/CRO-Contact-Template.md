I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to inquire about your capabilities and pricing for a preclinical neuroscience study involving porcine neural probe implantation.

### Study Overview

**Objective:** Evaluate long-term (90-day) stability and biocompatibility of Laser-Induced Graphene (LIG) neural probes in a porcine model.

**Study Design:**
- **Species:** Yucatan minipig (n=6, 3M/3F)
- **Age/Weight:** 6-8 months, 25-35 kg
- **Implant Site:** Primary motor cortex (M1) + primary somatosensory cortex (S1)
- **Device:** LIG neural probe (4 channels × 2 sites per animal)
- **Control:** Commercial neural probe (Blackrock NeuroPort)
- **Duration:** 90 days with weekly recordings
- **Endpoints:** 
  - Neural signal quality (SNR, impedance)
  - Histopathology (H&E, Iba1, GFAP)
  - MRI at 60 days
  - Terminal necropsy at 90 days

### Services Requested

1. Animal procurement and housing (SPF facility)
2. Stereotaxic implantation surgery
3. Post-operative care and monitoring
4. Weekly electrophysiological recordings
5. MRI imaging (60 days)
6. Terminal histology and immunohistochemistry
7. Data analysis and report preparation

### Timeline

- **Study Start:** [Target Date, e.g., April 2026]
- **Study Duration:** 16 weeks (including acclimation)
- **Final Report:** 4 weeks after study completion

4. Histopathology services
5. Project management
